The Story

From the UK
to the Top

Tyron Ash grew up in the UK with no clear direction and no one showing him what success looked like. He made wrong choices. In 2015, those choices caught up with him. He served 40 months in prison.

Inside, he made a decision. Not a vague wish. A decision. He studied, he planned, and he committed to building something real the moment he walked out. He was not going to be a statistic. He was going to be an example.

He came out and went straight to work in real estate. In 2016 he joined Fine and Country North London, one of the most respected luxury agencies in the country. He learned fast. He outperformed. He built his reputation from scratch, deal by deal, client by client. In 2018 he left Fine and Country to start building something of his own.

The Rise

Built from
Nothing

In 2019, Tyron Ash International Real Estate was born. No funding. No connections. No favours. Just a relentless focus on building a brand that stood for something real in luxury property.

It grew fast. Tyron became the number one most followed real estate brand on Instagram in both the UK and Dubai. He appeared on Channel 4's Mega Mansion Hunters, one of the most-watched property shows in the country. The brand expanded into Dubai, one of the fastest moving prime property markets on the planet.

Today, Tyron Ash International has a team of over 150 agents in the UK and 45 in Dubai. Tyron himself is a millionaire entrepreneur and investor based in Dubai. He did all of it without a background in business, without a network, and without a single door being opened for him.

The Business Today

Prime and
Super-Prime

Tyron Ash International operates across prime and super-prime luxury residential sales in both the UK and the UAE. The head office sits at Berkeley Square, Mayfair, London, one of the most prestigious business addresses in the world. The Dubai operation places the brand at the centre of one of the fastest growing property markets anywhere on earth.

The business runs on uncapped commission. Top agents within the group are earning £200,000 or more annually. Performance is rewarded without a ceiling.

Training is not an afterthought. It sits at the core of everything. Two to three hours every single week dedicated to prospecting, winning listing appointments, objection handling, and closing. The same systems taught inside the Blueprint are the systems the team runs on every day.
